faqs

a few helpful answers

what if i don’t know what to write?

start with the ordinary things: your favorite song, the people you spend time with, what you’re looking forward to. you don’t need to be a writer, and your letter doesn’t need to be profound.

what’s included?

your ticket includes space to write, one drink of your choice, stationery, a pen, an envelope, postage, and a polaroid photo to keep or tuck into your letter.

do i need to bring anything?

just yourself. we’ll provide the stationery and a pen to write with.

what if i can no longer attend?

tickets are non-refundable and non-exchangeable, but you’re welcome to transfer yours to someone else.

moving before your letter arrives?

we’ll contact you 4 weeks before your letter is mailed and you’ll have a chance to update your mailing address.
